;=============================================
; No filler is inserted when front matter already leaves lesson 1 recto.
;=============================================
GIVEN the recto guarantee is enabled and front matter whose length leaves lesson 1 on a right-hand page.
WHEN the book is assembled.
THEN no blank page is inserted.

;=============================================
; Exactly one blank page is inserted when front matter would leave lesson 1 verso.
;=============================================
GIVEN the recto guarantee is enabled and front matter whose length would leave lesson 1 on a left-hand page.
WHEN the book is assembled.
THEN exactly one blank page is inserted before lesson 1.

;=============================================
; The inserted blank page carries no content or number but consumes a front-matter number.
;=============================================
GIVEN the recto guarantee is enabled and an inserted blank page.
WHEN the reader looks at it.
THEN it carries no content and prints no page number.
THEN it consumes one number in the front-matter sequence.

;=============================================
; Lesson 1 opens recto in both assembly modes.
;=============================================
GIVEN the recto guarantee is enabled and either assembly mode, bilingual or single-language.
WHEN the book is assembled.
THEN lesson 1 opens recto in both modes.
